Chisel is built on a simple metaphor: a precise, durable tool used to shape raw material into something useful. In our case, the material is **information**.

## Our Philosophy

### 1. Human-first, AI-compatible
Tools should be delightful for human developers to use in a high-performance TUI, but structured enough that an LLM can consume and act on their output without "screen scraping" or parsing complex HTML.

### 2. The Terminal is Universal
The terminal remains the most stable, universal interface for both humans and machines. Chisel embraces this by staying terminal-native.

### 3. Local-first, Cloud-optional
Your data belongs to you. Chisel stores everything in plain text (Markdown/YAML) in your repository. This ensures it works offline, is git-backed, and is always accessible to your local AI agents.

### 4. Precision over Hype
We build tools that quietly do their job well. No unnecessary fluff, just fast, reliable utilities for shaping your workflow.
